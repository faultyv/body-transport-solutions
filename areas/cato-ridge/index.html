Why Cato Ridge works as a dispatch base

Cato Ridge sits on the N3 logistics corridor between Durban and Pietermaritzburg, which makes it practical for direct refrigerated human-remains transport across central KwaZulu-Natal. The location supports quick route decisions while keeping the service focused on collect-and-deliver transport.

The transport desk confirms who is instructing the job, where collection will happen, which facility will receive, what documentation is ready and whether the vehicle can be dispatched for the requested time.
