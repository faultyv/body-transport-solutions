Direct transport for Hillcrest providers

Hillcrest and Upper Highway requests can involve hospitals, mortuaries, funeral parlours, insurers and repatriation coordinators. Our transport desk confirms the authorised instruction, collection point, receiving facility, timing and documentation before dispatching the dedicated refrigerated vehicle.

Urgent and after-hours requests are assessed on call. Longer inter-provincial transfers are arranged in advance so the collection and delivery windows are clear.

Transport-only disclaimer: We do not store, prepare, embalm, cremate, bury or arrange funerals. The responsible authorised provider remains responsible for release documents, permits, declarations and funeral arrangements.
