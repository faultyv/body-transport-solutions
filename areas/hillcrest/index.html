Support for Hillcrest and Upper Highway facilities

Hillcrest and Kloof sit on the route between Cato Ridge and Durban, which keeps response times short for hospitals, mortuaries and funeral parlours in the area. Our transport-only model stays focused: collect under instruction, transport in a dedicated refrigerated vehicle, complete handover at the receiving point.

We can also connect Hillcrest routes onward into Durban, Pinetown or back toward Pietermaritzburg where a longer route is required.

  • Hillcrest, Kloof, Waterfall and Upper Highway support.
  • Fast onward connection to Durban and Pinetown.
  • Useful for private hospital release work and funeral operator overflow.

Transport-only disclaimer: Body Transport Solutions does not operate as a funeral parlour, mortuary, storage facility, embalming service, crematorium, burial service or funeral policy provider. All accepted assignments are direct refrigerated transport jobs subject to applicable documentation.
