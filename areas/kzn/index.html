One KZN network, connected to the actual route

KwaZulu-Natal is not a single urban route. A transport request can connect a hospital, clinic, mortuary, funeral parlour, insurer or repatriation coordinator to a receiving facility across municipal and health-district boundaries. This hub keeps those relationships connected without pretending that a road name or town alone is enough to confirm a job.

The official KZN Department of Health directory recognises health-district contexts including eThekwini, uMgungundlovu, Ugu, iLembe, Amajuba, Harry Gwala, uMkhanyakude, uMzinyathi, King Cetshwayo, uThukela and Zululand. eThekwini’s official area information identifies N2, N3 and King Shaka International Airport as important connections. We use this public geography as context; each accepted assignment still depends on the exact collection point, destination, timing, instruction and documentation.

Frequently asked questions

Which areas have dedicated pages?

Cato Ridge, Durban, Pinetown, Hillcrest and Pietermaritzburg currently have dedicated route pages. They are helpful entry points, not a limit on wider KZN route assessment.

Can you assess a route outside these pages?

Yes. Send the exact collection and receiving facilities, timing, instruction and documentation status. Wider KZN and longer inter-provincial transfers are assessed in advance.

Is this a funeral parlour or mortuary network?

No. Body Transport Solutions is a transport-only operator. We provide refrigerated movement and handover documentation, not storage, preparation or funeral arrangements.
