How to use this location network

This is a geographic route index, not a directory of hospitals, mortuaries or funeral homes. Body Transport Solutions provides direct, refrigerated facility-to-facility movement for authorised organisations. A location name is one routing signal; dispatch still needs the exact collection point, destination, timing, instructing party and documentation status.

Our operating context is the Cato Ridge and Camperdown N3 corridor, with KZN-wide assessments and longer inter-provincial transfers by arrangement. The official South African government KZN municipal directory and KZN Health Districts provide public administrative context; the geographic names below are cross-checked against GeoNames.

Transport-only disclaimer: We do not operate a mortuary, store human remains, prepare remains, arrange funerals or claim affiliation with any facility listed or located near a place name.

Frequently asked questions

Does every listed place have a dedicated Body Transport Solutions branch?

No. The directory is a geographic route-planning index. We confirm the exact collection facility, receiving facility, timing, instruction, documents and vehicle availability before dispatch.

Can you assess a route outside these 300+ entries?

Yes. Send the exact facilities and we will assess wider KZN routes and longer inter-provincial movement by arrangement.

What information should an authorised provider send?

Send the place or suburb, collection facility, destination facility, requested date and time, instructing organisation, receiving contact and documentation status.
