Planning a Newcastle or Northern KZN route

Newcastle sits well outside our immediate response radius, so Northern KZN work is handled as a scheduled, quoted route rather than an on-demand dispatch. That means confirming the collection facility, destination, instructing provider and documentation ahead of the transfer, then locking in a departure window.

Newcastle's location near the Free State and Gauteng borders also makes it a practical staging point for inter-provincial legs, arranged in advance with the required documentation.

  • Newcastle, Ladysmith and wider Northern KZN, by arrangement.
  • Route quoted and scheduled in advance of collection.
  • Onward inter-provincial legs (Free State, Gauteng) arranged where required.

Transport-only disclaimer: Body Transport Solutions is not a funeral parlour or mortuary. We do not store, prepare, embalm, cremate, bury, arrange funerals or sell policies. We provide direct refrigerated transport subject to documentation and legal requirements.
