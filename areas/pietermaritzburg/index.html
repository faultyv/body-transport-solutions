Support for Pietermaritzburg facilities and providers

Pietermaritzburg routes often connect hospitals, mortuaries, funeral parlours and longer KZN corridors. Our transport-only model keeps the work focused: collect under instruction, transport in a dedicated refrigerated vehicle, and complete handover at the receiving point.

The service can support local transfers, Durban connections, Midlands movements and inter-provincial routes by arrangement where documentation and receiving-party readiness are confirmed.

  • Pietermaritzburg, Hilton, Howick, Camperdown and Cato Ridge corridor support.
  • Scheduled and urgent transport requests assessed by dispatch.
  • Useful for funeral operator overflow, hospital release and mortuary transfer work.

Transport-only disclaimer: Body Transport Solutions does not operate as a funeral parlour, mortuary, storage facility, embalming service, crematorium, burial service or funeral policy provider. All accepted assignments are direct refrigerated transport jobs subject to applicable documentation.
