Transport support for Pinetown operators

Pinetown assignments can involve hospitals, mortuaries, funeral parlours, insurers and repatriation coordinators. Our role is narrow: confirm the authorised instruction, collect from the agreed point, move directly in the dedicated refrigerated vehicle and complete the receiving handover.

Urgent and after-hours requests are assessed on call. Acceptance depends on documentation, route readiness, vehicle availability and the receiving party.

Transport-only disclaimer: We do not store, prepare, embalm, cremate, bury or arrange funerals. The responsible authorised provider remains responsible for release documents, permits, declarations and funeral arrangements.
