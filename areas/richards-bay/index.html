Planning a Richards Bay or North Coast route

Because Richards Bay sits well outside our immediate response radius, we treat North Coast work as a scheduled, quoted route rather than an on-demand dispatch. That means confirming the collection facility, destination, instructing provider and documentation ahead of the transfer, then locking in a departure window.

This approach keeps the same transport-only standard — refrigerated compartment, signed handover, chain-of-custody records — over a longer distance.

  • Richards Bay, Empangeni and the wider North Coast, by arrangement.
  • Route quoted and scheduled in advance of collection.
  • Suited to hospital release, mortuary transfer and repatriation-linked routes.

Transport-only disclaimer: Body Transport Solutions is not a funeral parlour or mortuary. We do not store, prepare, embalm, cremate, bury, arrange funerals or sell policies. We provide direct refrigerated transport subject to documentation and legal requirements.
