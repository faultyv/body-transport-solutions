A clear transport role

Our service supports the practical conveyance requirements around a transfer. The instructing funeral parlour, mortuary, hospital, insurer or repatriation coordinator remains responsible for the lawful release, permits, receiving-party arrangements and any preparation or funeral service.

Our vehicle is configured for refrigerated transport, with the compartment set to the steady cold temperature required for the transfer, up to 3 °C. The route is handled as one accountable movement between authorised points.

Transport-only disclaimer: We do not store, prepare, embalm, cremate, bury or arrange funerals, and this page is not legal advice. Requirements can vary by route, facility and responsible authority. Confirm the applicable documentation before dispatch.

What should be ready before collection?

Send the collection facility, destination, instructing organisation, timing, receiving contact and documentation status so dispatch can assess the route.

Do you certify legal compliance?

No. We record the transport activity and follow the instruction supplied by authorised parties; legal responsibility remains with the relevant responsible authority.
