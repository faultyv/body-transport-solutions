## Google Business Profile

**Category**
- Primary: Transportation service

**Business description** (750 char limit):

> Body Transport Solutions provides dedicated refrigerated human-remains
> transport across KwaZulu-Natal for funeral parlours, mortuaries, hospitals,
> insurers and repatriation coordinators. We collect from authorised
> facilities and deliver directly to the receiving mortuary or funeral
> parlour in a purpose-built refrigerated vehicle, with signed handover
> records at every transfer. Transport-only — we do not store, prepare,
> embalm, cremate, bury or arrange funerals. Based in Cato Ridge on the N3,
> serving Durban, Pietermaritzburg and wider KZN, with inter-provincial
> routes by arrangement. Dispatch available 24/7 including after-hours and
> weekends.

**Services to list:**
1. Refrigerated body transport — Direct facility-to-facility transport in a temperature-controlled vehicle.
2. Human remains repatriation — Ground transport leg for inter-provincial and repatriation routes, coordinated with the authorised provider.
3. Funeral home overflow transport — Case-by-case refrigerated transport capacity when a funeral operator's own vehicle is unavailable.
4. After-hours & weekend dispatch — Urgent transport requests assessed and dispatched around the clock.

**Service area:** Service-area business (not storefront) — Cato Ridge, Camperdown, Durban, Pietermaritzburg, Hillcrest, Pinetown, Richards Bay, Port Shepstone, Newcastle. Decide deliberately whether to show the street address publicly, since there's no walk-in facility.

**Pre-seed the Q&A section:**
- *"Do you collect from hospitals?"* → "Yes — collection from hospitals, clinics and other authorised facilities is the core of what we do, with direct delivery to the receiving mortuary or funeral parlour."
- *"Are you a funeral parlour?"* → "No, we're transport-only. We don't store, prepare, embalm or arrange funerals — we move human remains between authorised facilities."
- *"Do you operate after hours?"* → "Yes, dispatch is available 24/7 including weekends. Availability depends on documentation, route and vehicle readiness."
