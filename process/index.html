How refrigerated human remains transport works

Our KZN transport process is designed for professional providers that need a clear handover sequence: instruction, readiness checks, refrigerated collection, direct movement, receiving sign-off and job close-out.

Send a transport briefWhatsApp dispatch

01. Instruct

Confirm the authorised provider, collection point, destination, timing and documentation status.

02. Prepare

Dispatch checks route readiness and prepares the dedicated refrigerated vehicle for the assignment.

03. Transfer

Collect under instruction, maintain the required cold setting and move directly to the receiving point.

04. Handover

Complete the receiving sign-off and close the job with route and cleaning records.

Transport-only disclaimer: We do not store, prepare or arrange funerals. The authorised provider remains responsible for release, permits, receiving-party arrangements and any required preparation.

Can urgent routes be requested?

Yes. Send the route and documentation status to dispatch; acceptance depends on readiness, scheduling and vehicle availability.

Is this a storage or mortuary process?

No. Each accepted assignment is direct collection and delivery between authorised points.
