What happens when a request comes in after-hours

An after-hours or weekend request goes through the same steps as any other: confirm the instructing provider, collection facility, destination facility, and documentation status. What changes outside business hours is mostly logistics — whether the receiving facility can accept a delivery at that time, and whether the vehicle is positioned for a fast turnaround.

Being ready with the full checklist (see below) matters even more after-hours, since there are fewer people available on either end to fill in missing details quickly.

  • Urgent requests can be sent to the dispatch line at any time via call or WhatsApp.
  • Collection is confirmed against documentation, route distance and vehicle readiness, not against the clock alone.
  • Funeral operators can use this as overflow capacity when their own after-hours resource is stretched.

Transport-only disclaimer: This page describes our operational approach to timing, not a guarantee of collection within a specific window. Legal responsibility for release and receiving-party arrangements remains with the authorised instructing party.

Is the dispatch line staffed 24 hours?

The dispatch line accepts requests at any time. Acceptance and timing still depend on documentation, route distance and vehicle availability.

Are weekend requests treated differently to weekday requests?

The assessment process is the same. What changes is scheduling: weekend and after-hours routes may take slightly longer to confirm depending on receiving-facility availability.
