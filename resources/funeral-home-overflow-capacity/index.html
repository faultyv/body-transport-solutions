Using overflow transport capacity as a funeral operator

Every funeral operator hits moments where their own vehicle isn't available, isn't suited to the route, or two collections land at once. Overflow transport capacity is a way to cover that gap without turning work away or delaying a family.

Where overflow capacity fits

The arrangement is simple: the funeral operator remains the instructing party and keeps the family relationship, and we handle the physical collection, refrigerated transfer and delivery as a transport-only assignment. It's designed to be used only when needed — not a standing contract.

This tends to come up around vehicle downtime, overlapping collections, a route outside the operator's normal coverage, or after-hours work when the operator's own driver isn't available.

  • Used case-by-case, not as a replacement for an operator's own fleet.
  • Instructing operator retains the family relationship and arrangement.
  • Signed handover and route records supplied for the operator's own file.

Transport-only disclaimer: Body Transport Solutions does not provide funeral arrangement, embalming, storage or any service beyond transport. All work is carried out under the instruction of the authorised operator or facility.

When do funeral homes typically use overflow transport?

Common situations include a vehicle being in service or unavailable, two collections needed at once, or a route further than the operator normally covers.

Does using overflow capacity affect who the family deals with?

No. We operate behind the scenes as the transport layer; the funeral operator remains the point of contact for the family and retains the arrangement.
