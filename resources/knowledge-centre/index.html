Start with the transport boundary

Body Transport Solutions is a dedicated transport operator. We move human remains directly between authorised collection and receiving facilities using a refrigerated vehicle and a documented handover. The company does not provide storage, preparation, embalming, funeral arrangements or family administration.

This distinction helps hospitals, mortuaries, funeral parlours, insurers and repatriation coordinators brief the correct service. For the regulatory context, refer to the South African Government notice for the Regulations on the Management of Human Remains (R363). For deaths abroad and consular guidance, use DIRCO’s deaths-abroad guidance. These links are reference sources, not a substitute for the responsible provider’s legal or municipal advice.

Transport-only disclaimer: The instructing and receiving authorised parties remain responsible for release documents, permits, declarations, identification, receiving arrangements and any funeral or family services.

Route brief: four useful steps

  1. Confirm the collection point. Note the authorised facility and release contact.
  2. Confirm the destination. Note the receiving facility and handover contact.
  3. Share timing and documents. Include the requested window, instructing organisation and documentation status.
  4. Request an assessment. Dispatch can then consider the route, availability and handover requirements.

Frequently asked questions

What does transport-only mean?

We provide the vehicle, direct collection-to-delivery movement and transport records for accepted assignments. We do not store, prepare or arrange funerals.

How is the refrigeration setting chosen?

The compartment is pre-cooled, set and monitored to the controlled cold condition required for the transfer. The operating setting is agreed for the assignment rather than advertised as a one-size-fits-all number.

Can you help with a longer repatriation route?

Longer domestic and inter-provincial transfers can be assessed in advance. The authorised provider remains responsible for the permits, declarations and receiving arrangements.
