Frequently asked questions

Can this hub replace a procurement specification?

No. It helps a buyer frame the transport scope and questions. The purchasing organisation must use its current RFQ, tender, SLA and compliance requirements.

What is the shortest useful first message?

Send: “Collection [facility], destination [facility], requested time [window], instructing organisation [name].” Dispatch can then ask for the missing route and document details.

Why is the location list not a branch directory?

The location index is route context. It does not imply a depot, mortuary, hospital affiliation or guaranteed response in each listed place.
