Geography is a workflow, not a list

A useful local entity relationship is: authorised collection facility → refrigerated transport unit → receiving facility → signed handover. Roads, municipalities and health districts add context to that relationship; they do not replace the exact collection and delivery details.

The company is based in the Cato Ridge and Camperdown corridor. eThekwini describes its metropolitan area as connected by the N2, N3 and King Shaka International Airport, while the KwaZulu-Natal Department of Health publishes district-level facility and municipality context. Those official references help explain the network, but each accepted assignment is still assessed from its real collection point and destination.

What makes a route commercially useful?

For transport planning, the strongest opportunity signal is not population alone. It is the combination of a clear authorised instruction, a defined collection and destination, a realistic timing window, documentation readiness and a receiving contact. Corridor access and repeat facility relationships can improve operational fit, but no page can responsibly promise availability, price or response time without the actual brief.

  1. Origin: exact facility and release contact.
  2. Destination: receiving facility and handover contact.
  3. Timing: requested collection window and urgency.
  4. Readiness: responsible organisation and documentation status.

Frequently asked questions

Does this page list every KZN facility?

No. It is an operational route model, not a public directory or affiliation claim. The instructing party confirms the facilities for each assignment.

Can a road name alone produce a dispatch quote?

No. Dispatch needs the exact collection and receiving points, timing, authorised instruction and documentation status.

Do you cover routes outside the listed area pages?

Wider KZN and longer inter-provincial requests can be assessed. Send the complete route brief so the transport desk can check fit and availability.
