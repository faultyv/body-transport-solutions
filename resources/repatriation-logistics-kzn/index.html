Ground movement, one leg of a larger process

Where transport-only support fits in repatriation logistics

Repatriation, whether inter-provincial or international, involves several parties: the instructing funeral home or coordinator, the authorities issuing documentation, and the transport leg that physically moves the remains between points. This page explains where our role starts and stops.

What the transport leg actually covers

Once an authorised coordinator has confirmed the instruction — collection point, destination, timing and documentation status — our part is straightforward: collect in the refrigerated vehicle, transport directly to the agreed point, and complete a signed handover. For inter-provincial routes that connect to onward repatriation arrangements, we coordinate timing with the receiving party so the handover lines up.

We don't issue or process the permits, consular paperwork or cross-border documentation that repatriation typically requires. That stays with the funeral home, coordinator or authority managing the case; our role is limited to the physical movement once their instruction is confirmed.

  • Local KZN collection through to a provincial handover point.
  • Onward inter-provincial routes by arrangement (Gauteng, Free State, Eastern Cape, Mpumalanga).
  • Scheduling coordinated with the receiving facility or onward provider.

Transport-only disclaimer: This page describes our operational role only and is not legal or immigration advice. Permits, consular processes and receiving-party arrangements remain the responsibility of the authorised coordinator.

Do you coordinate permits and documentation for repatriation?

No. Permits, consular or cross-border documentation and receiving-party arrangements are handled by the authorised coordinator or provider. We handle the ground transport leg once instructed.

Can you collect from or deliver to an airport or port of entry?

Yes, subject to the coordinator's confirmed instruction, timing and any facility access requirements at that point.
