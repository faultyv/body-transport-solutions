Before you contact dispatch

What to prepare before requesting refrigerated transport

A complete request lets dispatch assess route, documentation and vehicle readiness in one pass instead of several back-and-forth messages. Here's what to have ready before you call or WhatsApp the transport desk.

The six things dispatch will ask for

  • Instructing provider. The funeral parlour, mortuary, hospital, insurer or repatriation coordinator authorising the transfer.
  • Collection facility. Name, address and a contact person who can confirm release.
  • Destination facility. Name, address and the receiving contact who will sign for handover.
  • Timing. A requested collection window, or whether the request is urgent and needs same-day assessment.
  • Documentation status. Whether release paperwork is ready, pending, or being finalised by the instructing party.
  • Route context. Anything unusual about access, distance or onward connections that affects scheduling.

Sending these details together, rather than one message at a time, is the single biggest thing that speeds up a response — especially for after-hours or weekend requests.

Transport-only disclaimer: This checklist covers what dispatch needs operationally. It is not legal advice, and the instructing party remains responsible for the lawful release, permits and receiving-party arrangements required for the transfer.

What's the fastest way to send a transport request?

WhatsApp or call the dispatch line directly with the checklist details above. Having collection, destination and documentation status ready speeds up the assessment.

Can a request be submitted before all documentation is ready?

You can start the conversation early, but dispatch will confirm documentation status before the vehicle is scheduled for collection.
