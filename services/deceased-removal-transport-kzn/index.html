Plain language for a regulated transport task

Funeral parlours and mortuaries may refer to a job as a removal, body collection, deceased transfer or mortuary transfer. The formal term used in Regulation R363 is the conveyance of human remains. We use the language that professional teams recognise while keeping the service scope clear: direct refrigerated movement from the authorised collection point to the authorised receiving point.

Our dispatch desk supports funeral parlours, mortuaries, hospitals, insurers and repatriation coordinators. We confirm the route, timing, vehicle readiness and documents supplied by the responsible provider before accepting the assignment.

  • Cato Ridge / Camperdown N3 base with Durban, Pietermaritzburg and wider KZN access.
  • Urgent and after-hours requests assessed on call, subject to readiness and availability.
  • Signed collection and receiving records, with vehicle cleaning logs.
  • Inter-provincial and repatriation ground transfers arranged in advance.

Transport-only disclaimer: We do not operate as a funeral parlour, mortuary, embalming facility, crematorium, burial service or funeral policy provider. We do not store, prepare or arrange funerals. The responsible authorised parties remain responsible for release documents, permits, declarations and any preparation or funeral arrangements.

Is “body removal” the same service?

It can describe the same collection-and-transfer task in everyday search language. The formal service is transport of human remains between authorised points.

Can families book a removal directly?

Families may contact us for guidance, but an authorised provider, facility release and receiving handover must be confirmed before a transport job is accepted.
