Hospital-to-mortuary transport in KwaZulu-Natal

When a deceased person needs to move from a hospital or clinic to a receiving mortuary, Body Transport Solutions provides the refrigerated transport between the authorised collection and delivery points. In funeral-industry language this is often called a removal or transfer.

A practical hospital-to-mortuary transport layer

The transport desk confirms the hospital or clinic collection point, the receiving mortuary, the instructing organisation, the requested timing, the receiving contact and the documentation status. The vehicle is then prepared for a direct refrigerated transfer and the receiving party signs the handover record at delivery.

This service is intended for funeral parlours, hospitals, mortuaries, insurers and authorised repatriation coordinators. Families may contact us for guidance, but an authorised provider and facility release process must be in place before transport can be accepted.

  • Durban, Pietermaritzburg, Cato Ridge and wider KZN facility transfers.
  • Urgent and after-hours requests assessed on call against route readiness and vehicle availability.
  • Vehicle cleaning records and signed collection and receiving notes.
  • Longer inter-provincial routes arranged in advance.

Transport-only disclaimer: Body Transport Solutions is not a hospital, mortuary, funeral parlour, embalming facility, crematorium or burial service. We do not store, prepare or arrange funerals. The responsible authorised parties remain responsible for release documents, permits, declarations and receiving arrangements.
