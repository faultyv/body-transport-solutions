Built for repatriation coordinators and authorised providers

Repatriation work often involves tight handover windows, receiving-party coordination and documentation checks. Our transport desk focuses on the practical transport sequence: collection instruction, facility release, route confirmation, refrigerated transfer and receiving sign-off.

Durban routes can include local movement, airport or route handover support where arranged by authorised parties, and longer inter-provincial transfers when documentation and delivery readiness are confirmed in advance.

  • Ground transport support for provider-led repatriation processes.
  • Direct refrigerated movement from Durban and wider KZN facilities.
  • Route planning around collection time, receiving facility readiness and documentation status.
  • Transport records, cleaning logs and signed handover documentation.

Transport-only disclaimer: We provide refrigerated transport only. We do not store human remains, prepare human remains, embalm, arrange funerals, sell policies or guarantee permit approval. Repatriation documentation and authorisations must be supplied by the responsible authorised parties.
