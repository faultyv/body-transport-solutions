A controlled transport service for KZN providers

When a facility needs human remains moved across KwaZulu-Natal, the work has to be practical, documented and discreet. Our role is the transport layer: confirm the instructing party, check the collection and destination details, confirm the route, prepare the refrigerated vehicle and complete the handover record at delivery.

This page is for professional teams searching for refrigerated body transport KwaZulu-Natal support without confusing it with funeral arrangement, mortuary storage or preparation services.

  • Local and long-distance KZN transfers between authorised facilities.

Who should contact the transport desk?

Funeral parlours, mortuaries, hospitals, insurers, repatriation coordinators and authorised providers can contact the dispatch line with collection, destination, timing and documentation status.

Do you do dead body collections from a hospital to a mortuary?

Yes. That's the core of the service: collection from an authorised facility such as a hospital, clinic or mortuary, refrigerated transport, and direct delivery to the receiving facility with signed handover records.

Can families contact you directly?

Yes, but the transport still needs the correct authorised provider, facility release and destination handover process in place.

Is the service available for urgent routes?

Urgent requests can be sent at any time. Acceptance depends on documentation, vehicle readiness, route distance and scheduling.
