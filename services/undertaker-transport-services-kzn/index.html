A body transport service built around the funeral trade

Most undertakers and funeral directors run one or two vehicles. That works until a second call comes in during a funeral, a driver is off, the vehicle is in for service, or a family needs someone collected three hours away. Our role is to absorb that specific problem: we take the transport leg, under your instruction, and hand back a signed record for your file.

We work as a subcontracted transport layer for the funeral services business that instructs us. The instructing undertaker or funeral director stays the point of contact for the family, retains the arrangement, and remains responsible for the lawful release, documentation and receiving-party arrangements. We do the driving and the cold chain, nothing else.

  • Collections from hospitals, clinics, mortuaries and private residences to your premises, under your instruction.
  • Branch-to-branch and parlour-to-parlour transfers for multi-branch funeral services businesses.
  • Overflow capacity when your own vehicle is committed, unavailable or unsuitable for the route.
  • Long-distance and inter-provincial legs quoted and scheduled in advance.

Transport-only disclaimer: Body Transport Solutions (Pty) Ltd is not an undertaker, funeral director, funeral parlour, mortuary, embalming facility, crematorium, burial service or funeral policy provider. We do not store, prepare or arrange funerals, and we do not solicit or serve families directly. Every accepted job is a direct collect-and-deliver transport assignment carried out under the written instruction of an authorised party, subject to applicable documentation and legal requirements.

Do you provide transport for undertakers and funeral directors?

Yes. Undertakers, funeral directors and funeral services businesses are our core customers. We provide the refrigerated transport leg between authorised facilities so your team can stay with the family and the arrangement.

Are you a funeral services provider yourself?

No. We are transport-only. We do not arrange funerals, store, prepare, embalm, cremate or bury, and we do not deal with families in place of the instructing undertaker or funeral director.

Can a funeral director use you only when needed?

Yes. There is no retainer or minimum volume. Most funeral directors use us case by case when their own vehicle is unavailable, two collections overlap, or a route falls outside their usual radius.
