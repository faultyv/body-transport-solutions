Purpose-built refrigerated unit

A transport vehicle built for controlled transfer

The dedicated vehicle combines a lift-up rear hatch, stainless-steel compartment, secure platform, forced-air circulation and easy-clean surfaces for direct human remains transport.

See the transport serviceRequest route capacity

Lift-up rear hatch

The rear hatch lifts from the top to provide a clear, enclosed access point for the secure transport platform.

Stainless compartment

Non-porous surfaces and a dedicated drainage line support practical cleaning and vehicle close-out after each assignment.

Cold control

The system is set and monitored for the controlled cold condition required for each transfer, rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all setting.

Transport-only disclaimer: Vehicle features describe the transport unit only. We do not store, prepare or arrange funerals, and accepted routes remain subject to documentation, scheduling and availability.
